What MANA does with it

A person sensed by Wi-Fi but not seen by the camera means someone may be round the corner or behind a door: slow down and say so. One source, so it slows and never stops (the core's rule for a single sensor). Proposed: not yet an input to the decision core.

What the receiver gets weakstrong

The strength of each of the 56 subcarriers, the last 10 seconds. A body in the room bends the pattern; movement makes it flicker.

How it works
  1. Two ESP32 boards (about 3 m apart): one sends Wi-Fi packets, the other reports each packet's channel state, about 200 a second.
  2. Each window of one second: how much every subcarrier flickers, and the shape of their strengths.
  3. A small linear model fitted on the room empty and with a person in each zone, run on the laptop with numpy.
